# Elsevier: Declaration of Interests

For Elsevier journals
(e.g. Expert Systems with Applications)
who require submitting a Declaration of Interests file (a .docx file),
you can:
1. Fill its website-based questionnaire at:
[Related Elsevier policies, documents and initiatives](https://www.elsevier.com/editor/perk/related-elsevier-policies-documents-and-initiatives)
->
[Declaration of Interests](https://declarations.elsevier.com/home)
->
`Start my declaration` (buttom at the lower-right corner).
2. Download it after the website hint on finish.
